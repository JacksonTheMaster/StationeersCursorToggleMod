[h1]CursorToggle – Double-Tap to Toggle Mouse Cursor[/h1]

[b]A StationeersLaunchPad mod that lets you double-tap the MouseControl key (default: Alt) to toggle the mouse cursor on/off, instead of having to hold it.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] Single tap (hold) Alt: vanilla hold-to-show behaviour
[*] Double-tap Alt: toggle cursor on permanently until you double-tap again
[*] Works with whatever key you have bound to MouseControl in the game settings
[*] Lightweight – only two small Harmony patches
[/list]

[h2]Installation[/h2]
[list]
[*] Install [b]BepInEx[/b] + [b]StationeersLaunchPad[/b] (required for Stationeers mods)
[*] Subscribe to the Mod in the Workshop
[*] Launch the game & double-tap your Mouse Control Key (default Alt) to toggle the cursor
[/list]

[h2]Usage[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Hold Alt[/b] (single tap 6 hold) – cursor appears while held, disappears on release (vanilla behaviour)
[*] [b]Double-tap Alt[/b] – cursor stays visible; double-tap again to hide it
[/list]
