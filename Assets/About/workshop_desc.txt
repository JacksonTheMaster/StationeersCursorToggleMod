[h1]CursorToggle – Toggle Mouse Cursor[/h1]

[b]A StationeersLaunchPad mod that lets you tap the MouseControl key (default: Alt) to toggle the mouse cursor on/off, instead of having to hold it.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Double-tap mode (default):[/b] double-tap Alt to toggle cursor on/off
[*] [b]Single-tap mode:[/b] one tap toggles the cursor on/off
[*] Switch between modes via the Mod Settings window (Main Menu -> Workshop -> CursorToggle -> Right side of screen, blue window)
[*] Works with whatever key you have bound to MouseControl in the game settings
[*] In Double-tap mode a single tap has vanilla behaviour
[*] Lightweight – only two small Harmony patches
[/list]

[h2]Installation[/h2]
[list]
[*] Install [b]BepInEx[/b] + [b]StationeersLaunchPad[/b] (required for Stationeers mods)
[*] Subscribe to the Mod in the Workshop
[*] Launch the game & tap your Mouse Control Key (default Alt) to toggle the cursor
[/list]

[h2]Usage[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Double-tap Alt[/b] (default) – cursor stays visible; double-tap again to hide it
[*] [b]Single-tap Alt[/b] (if configured) – one press toggles the cursor on/off
[/list]
